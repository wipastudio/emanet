2 · What we don't collect

  • Which apps you seal. Apple's Screen Time framework gives us anonymous markers, not app names. Those markers never leave your device; our server has no counterpart. We never read app names from iOS. If you choose to turn on "Let your keeper see which app" for a seal, only the label you type is sent with that request — off by default, and you can switch it off any time.
  • How long you use them. Emanet neither shows nor measures usage time.
  • Which websites you open.
  • Your location, contacts or photos.
  • Advertising identifiers. Emanet does no tracking, so the App Tracking Transparency prompt never appears.

If our server leaked, what could be learned: who is paired with whom, when seals were set, and what was written — plus the labels you chose to type. How long you use your phone, which sites you open, the list of apps iOS knows — none of it.

3 · Where it lives

Database, authentication and files run on Supabase in eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany). Backups stay in the same region.

One exception worth stating: the serverless functions ("Edge Functions") that send notifications and handle subscription events run on Supabase's global edge network, which may include nodes outside the EU. They store nothing; they process and forward to Apple's notification service.

Notifications go through the Apple Push Notification service (Apple Inc.). Subscriptions are verified on our own server using the App Store's signed transaction; we use no third-party subscription service.

These pages and invite links are published on GitHub Pages (wipastudio.github.io/emanet, GitHub Inc., USA). The pages use no cookies and contain no analytics or tracking code. The invite page asks our server about the code in the link so it can show the inviter's name; GitHub may keep standard server logs (IP address) when serving the page.

4 · Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Circle, seal, request, decision: performance of a contract (6(1)(b)).
  • Event log and product measurement: legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) — no personalised ads, no profiling.
  • IP log of failed invite attempts: legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) — preventing abuse; deleted after one day.
  • Sign in with Apple recovery: consent (6(1)(a)) — entirely optional.

6 · What the other person in your circle sees

Your keeper sees the sentence you write, how many minutes you asked for, until when your seal runs and whether it is working. If you turned on "Let your keeper see which app" for that seal, they also see the label you typed (e.g. "Deniz wants 20 minutes for Instagram"). Emergency unlocks never carry a label.

Your keeper does not see: which app you want to open on seals where the label is off, how long you use your phone, or what you do at what time. There is no behaviour report, and there won't be.

7 · Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR Arts. 15–22; KVKK Art. 11).

Account deletion is in the app: Settings → Delete account. You leave the circle (the circle is deleted if nobody else is left), every seal is lifted and your data is permanently deleted; we don't soft-delete.

Requests: wipastudios@gmail.com · You may complain to your EU supervisory authority or Turkey's Personal Data Protection Authority.

8 · Children

Emanet is not directed at children under 13. It uses Screen Time in the .individual mode — an adult restricting themselves — and is not a parental-control product.

9 · Changes

If we change this policy we update the date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

With Emanet you seal your apps. When you want one open, you write one sentence; the person you trust — your keeper — says "Yes" or "Not now" from their Lock Screen within 90 seconds.

Frequently asked

Can my keeper see which app I want to open?

Not by default. Apple's framework doesn't give us app names; your keeper only reads the sentence you write. If you like, turn on "Let your keeper see which app" for a seal: you then type the app's name in the request (or tap a quick pick) and your keeper sees "Deniz wants 20 minutes for Instagram". You can switch it off in Settings any time.

Is being a keeper paid?

No, never. There is no payment screen on the keeper side.

I tapped the invite link and installed from the App Store — where's the invite?

Open Emanet and tap "I have an invite link" on the first screen. Paste the link from the message or type the code.

What if my keeper doesn't answer?

After 90 seconds the request closes on its own and the seal holds — silence is not a yes. If three requests in a row go unanswered, your keeper gets a message asking whether notifications reach them, and you're offered a free backup keeper.

As a keeper I don't want to be disturbed at night.

Settings → Your duty hours. Outside them requests don't reach you; if there's a backup keeper they go there, otherwise the other person sees "off duty" and the seal holds. Emergency unlock is always available.

What do I do in a real emergency?

"Emergency unlock" is free and unlimited on every tier: it opens for 15 minutes. Your keeper is only told "used an emergency unlock".

I want the seal only on weekdays, or only at night.

When setting a seal choose "Custom · pick days and hours": you choose the days and hours, including ranges that cross midnight (e.g. 23:00 – 07:00). Custom hours and sealing with a Focus are part of the Pair subscription; the three preset rules are available on every tier.

How does sealing with a Focus work?

iPhone Settings → Focus → pick a Focus → Focus Filters → Emanet. While that Focus is on, the seal is on; when it ends, your rule takes over again.

Can I use Siri or Shortcuts?

Yes: "Ask your keeper" opens the app on the request screen; "Seal status" tells you whether the seal is on and until when.

My seals don't seem to work.

iOS occasionally refreshes app markers and the old ones stop working. Opening the app repairs most cases; otherwise you'll see a "pick again" screen.

My subscription ended — will my seals lift?

No. Existing seals work until the end of that week. After that you can continue on the free tier: one person in the circle, up to three apps.

How do I cancel?

Settings → Subscription → Manage → App Store. No survey, no retention offer.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete account. Your seals are lifted and your data is permanently deleted.

Is there an Android version?

No. Emanet is built on Apple's Screen Time framework, which has no Android counterpart. For now your keeper needs an iPhone too.

1 · What Emanet does and doesn't do

Emanet lets you seal apps with Apple's Screen Time framework and leave the decision to open one to a person you trust.

Emanet is not a parental-control product. You set the seal and you can lift it; your keeper only answers individual unlock requests.

Emanet is not a security product. Screen Time permission can be turned off in Settings, and the seal lifts at that moment. Apple's Screen Time passcode makes that step harder (and protects it on iOS 27) — but no configuration makes a lock absolute.

2 · Account

No sign-up, no password. An anonymous account is created when you open the app. Sign in with Apple is offered only for recovery. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

3 · Subscription

  • Being a keeper is free forever, including the weekly digest and trajectory. The keeper interface has no commercial messages.
  • The trial is 21 days, no card is taken and it never converts on its own; on day 21 you choose: the Pair subscription or the free tier.
  • On the free tier, one person in the circle can seal up to three apps; the three preset rules (every day, today, one week) are available on every tier. Pair adds sealing for both of you, up to 40 apps, your own days and hours and sealing with a Focus.
  • The trial starts when your partner accepts the invite, not at setup.
  • The subscription belongs to the circle, not the person.
  • Payment is taken through your App Store account. Cancel in App Store → Subscriptions; there is no friction in the app.
  • The price shown is the local price reported by the App Store.

When a subscription ends we don't lift your seals. Existing seals keep working until the end of that week; after that you can continue on the free tier. Your keeper role is never limited.

4 · Emergency unlock

Free and unlimited on every tier: it opens for 15 minutes and then seals again. Your keeper gets a plain notice; it isn't counted or questioned.

5 · What we expect from you

  • Request sentences and app labels are read by the other person. Don't write harassment, threats or unlawful content.
  • Don't use Emanet to restrict someone else without their consent. The product rests on mutual consent, and either side can end the circle unilaterally and instantly.
  • Don't reverse-engineer, automate or try to exceed service limits.
